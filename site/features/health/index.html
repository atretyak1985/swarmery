Features/03 Health

How your agents are doing, and what to change

Agents rarely fail loudly. See the pattern, change one thing.

A retry here, a lint error there. Health reduces a date range to one sentence about the fleet and the agents that moved it, names the friction you can remove today, and turns patterns into recommendations with the evidence attached.
